# Home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. A carver arrives from a link in a Danish carving group, mostly on a phone, and browses the trees to find one worth carving. Sets the replacement visual world for the whole site; the card, list, filter key and static pages follow in later passes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a wall of tree signs: big tree photos, each with an enamel name plate and round pictogram badges for its carving factors. It refuses the nature-app default of a forest hero over a grid of chip cards.

OWN-WORLD: A fixed enamel set of five, no tints between: cobalt for wayfinding plates, header and links; forest green for name plates; sign yellow for the action; signal red only for toxic species; white enamel ground. Gabarito on plates and headings, Figtree for text. Plates have rounded corners and an inset white keyline; badges are round; authored glyphs in one stroke weight. No gradients, no drop shadows.

STORY: The visitor recognises trees by their photos, sees at a glance which carve easily, how hard they are and whether they're food safe, and taps one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim cobalt header plate; a cobalt hero plate with the hook "Hvilket træ skal du snitte i?" and a working pill search with a yellow button; a row of round use badges that filter the wall; then a two-column wall of tree photos, each with its plate and four badges (green carving, dry hardness, food safety, leaf type). Signpost arrow plates below the wall lead to the filter key and to getting wood.

FORM: Scandinavian enamel signs, candidate 5 of 7 (re-roll 1, steered "a bit less bold, tree photos clearly on the home page"). seed:c8169133808f1f6b

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Raises

- One special finish, kept for the wordmark plate only.
- Every badge leads to the sourced line on the card that backs it (card pass).
- The use badge you pick fills; the others stay quiet.
- Colour comes from the fixed enamel set, never tints in between.
- Fixed cells: every tree's badges sit in the same order.

## Open decisions

- The species card still wears the field-guide world until its own pass.
