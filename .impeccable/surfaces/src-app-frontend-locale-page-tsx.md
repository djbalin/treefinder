# Home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. A carver arrives from a link in a Danish carving group, mostly on a phone, and browses the trees to find one worth carving. Sets the replacement visual world for the whole site; the card, list, filter key and static pages follow in later passes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a wall of tree photos, each with a flat enamel name band and round badges for its carving factors. It refuses the nature-app default of a forest hero over a grid of chip cards, and the kitsch of sign costume (keylines, rivets, arrow plates).

OWN-WORLD: Flat enamel colour, solid and untinted: an ink header bar, cobalt for links, actions, the picked use and one route, forest green for name bands, a food-safety check and the other route, signal red only for toxic species, on a white ground. No yellow (maintainer: too IKEA). Gabarito for names, nav and labels, Figtree for text. Square-cut tiles with a small radius, authored glyphs in one stroke weight, no keylines, gradients or shadows.

STORY: The visitor recognises trees by their photos, sees at a glance which carve easily, how hard they are and whether they're food safe, and taps one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim ink header bar with the links and the search, no logo; a small "Hvad vil du lave?" label over one horizontal row of compact use pills; a one-line badge legend (Snitbarhed, Hårdhed, Mad); then the wall, two columns on a phone and four on desktop, each tile a photo with a full-width green (red for toxic) band carrying the name in big type and three badges. The page heading is for screen readers only. Two flat route bars and the photo credits close the page.

FORM: Scandinavian enamel signs, candidate 5 of 7 (re-roll 1, steered "a bit less bold, tree photos clearly on the home page"). seed:c8169133808f1f6b

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Raises

- Every badge leads to the sourced line on the card that backs it (card pass).
- The use badge you pick fills; the others stay quiet.
- Colour comes from the fixed set, never tints in between.
- Fixed cells: every tree's badges sit in the same order.
